01 / Research

Inflammation, the brain
and recovery.

My current research asks how systemic TNF-α-driven inflammation relates to delirium vulnerability in ageing, and how recovery dynamics may be modulated by IL-17A.

This research focus is listed in the Trinity College Institute of Neuroscience’s 2026 Neuroscience Research Day programme. My work sits at the intersection of neuroimmunology, ageing and brain network function.

03 / About

Researcher by training.
Builder by curiosity.

I work in neuroimmunology at Trinity College Dublin, with earlier research experience at the National Centre for Biological Sciences in Bengaluru.

Before my current research, I worked as a Junior Research Fellow in Hiyaa Ghosh’s group at NCBS from 2021, studying microglial responses to injury. I hold an MSc in Biochemistry from the Central University of Kerala (2019–2021) and a BSc in Biochemistry from Hindi Mahavidyalaya (2016–2019).
