## Decorative artwork and typography

`assets/neural-constellation.webp` was generated specifically for this portfolio
with OpenAI Image Gen on 5 October 2026 and encoded from its original PNG.
It is abstract decoration, not a microscopy image, brain model or scientific
result. The interactive constellation is also procedural decoration.

Manrope is self-hosted from the Google Fonts distribution with its OFL licence
at `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`. Three.js and Embla use MIT licences, copied to
`assets/third-party-licenses.txt` and included in the static build.
