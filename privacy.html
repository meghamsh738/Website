Privacy note

This is a static personal portfolio. It has no account system, visitor-submission form, analytics code, advertising or embedded third-party media.

Email and external links

The email link opens your email application; any message you send is handled by your email provider and delivered to the address shown on the site. Links to GitHub, Trinity College Dublin, Oxford Academic and other services take you to sites operated by their respective providers.

Hosting

The portfolio is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub’s own service and privacy information applies to requests made to that host. This note describes the features present in the portfolio code and is not a statement about data collected by third-party services.

Interactive media

Project images, silent workflow recordings, the decorative scene and fonts are served with this site. Videos load when you choose Play. The motion control affects this page only; its setting is not stored or sent anywhere.
